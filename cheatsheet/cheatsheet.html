The goal: every book on the returns cart has a wish. Put every book on the shelf so that all of them are happy 💖. The shelves have exactly enough room: every slot gets a book, so every placement affects someone else.

1Controls

👆Tap a book to read its wish and rules.
🖐️Drag it to a slot (or tap the book, then an empty slot).
🔄Drop on another book to swap them.
📦Drag back to the cart to take a book off the shelf.
😊While dragging, the book's face shows if it would be happy there.
💡Hint puts one book where it belongs (Library: 5 💧).

4Pro tips

🎯Place the pickiest books first: 🪟 🤫 ⬆️ ⬇️ 👑 🙈, golden books and anything with 2+ icons.
🔗👉 and 👇/👆 books form chains. Place them as pairs.
🧮Count genres: three 👑 books of the same genre need three different shelves.
🙈"No neighbors" on a full shelf only works next to decor or a wall.
👀After every move, look for new 💢. Neighbors get upset too!
🔥Happy placements in a row build a combo, so plan before you drop.

5Decor & drama

🍵Teacup. Always warm. Somehow.
🍪Snack jar. Crumbs are a love language.
💡Lamp. For books with very small fonts.
🪴Fern. Its name is Gerald.
🐈Cat. Tap to pet (+1 💧/day). Knocks books off shelves.
Decor takes up a slot and can't be moved. Leave a book grumpy and it will complain, argue with neighbors, sneeze at the cat, get a 🌧️ and finally throw a tantrum.
